1 · Brand base

Cream is the paper, green is the primary, pink is the accent. Neutral surfaces stay warm cream tints; the dark appearance is a deep basil-black so the cream and green still read as one family. Pink has one token (--brand-pink) with its own dark-mode line.

Contrast, every pairing used (light and dark)

Text pairs need 4.5:1; graphics and controls 3:1 (WCAG 2.2). “n/a” means the pair is intentionally not used.

2 · Where the pink goes now (v4), and where it never goes

The owner could hardly see the pink in v3 (only five of the 23 screens had any, and only the Home had a pink shape bigger than a few pixels). v4 makes it the brand sparkle: small bold shapes that repeat on nearly every screen, while the app still reads as cream and green. Each row is one token in _src/tokens.css section 3b; the contrast numbers are computed live from those tokens.

“State” rows carry a state (current step, selected chip, progress), so they need 3:1 against their ground and never stand alone: each has a second signal (the words “Step 2 of 5”, the count “6 of 8”, a filled thumb, an avatar ring). “Decor” rows are illustration and decoration. Pink on the green button is 1.56:1, so the button never carries pink itself: the ÷ sits on a white (dark: near-black) chip.

Pink at a glance (the live pages)

USEShapes, icons and decoration: the ÷ motif, progress dots and bars, selected-state underlines and rails, the primary button chip, camera corners and shutter ring, Chavito's nose, cheeks, tongue and coin, the success disc and confetti. USELarge soft discs: behind Chavito (Home, camera denied, success). NEVERSmall text. Pink is 3.15:1 on cream (5.3:1 on the dark paper). If text ever needs the accent it uses --pink-ink (5.1:1 on paper). Large bold text may be pink only where it meets 3:1; nothing is today. NEVERWhere warn belongs: unassigned rows, mismatch banners, the failed-scan and remove-person screens keep their red-orange; pink never fills a row or banner, never sits beside the warning glyph, and is never dashed. The only dashed pink thing is the camera's receipt-edge guide, which is on a dark viewfinder far from any warning. NEVERPerson avatars and the share bar segments. A person must never look like the brand or like an error. The thin pink rule under the share bar is separate from the segments. NEVERText on a pink fill or pink on the green button (1.56:1). The only text on pink is the “1¢” on Chavito's coin and the check in the “just assigned” badge, both small illustration marks (5.3:1 in dark ink). NEVERThe OK state. “Adds up” banners and ✓ marks stay green: green means done and correct, pink means look here, or celebrate.

2b · The wordmark: the acute on the É

ESPLITÉALO is the logo's outlined wordmark (Unkempt Bold) with the acute as its own path, so it takes --wm-accent (the pink, with its dark value) while the letters take --wm-ink (the normal title ink). The real text “Esplitéalo” stays in the page, invisible, for copy, search and screen readers. The logo lockup draws the letters in green; to match it exactly, point --wm-ink at var(--primary) (one line).

3 · Person colors: the palette

Eight muted, earthy tones for the letter avatars, chosen to harmonize with cream, green and pink without ever being them. Every slot has a light-mode fill (white letter) and a dark-mode tint (dark letter). They were found by a constrained search (hue families, chroma limits, letter contrast, distance from the brand colors, and the color-vision simulations below) and then checked by eye. Slot order puts the most different colors first, because most bills have 2 to 4 people.

Under color-vision deficiency (simulation, Machado 2009, severity 1.0)

A simulation, not a test with people. The avatar always shows an initial and a name, so color is never the only signal; the table shows the closest pairs so you know where it is hardest.

6 · Share sheet plan

The share sheet follows the brand and the person palette: a cream surface, the one green primary action, neutral tiles for the other apps, person colors only on the little avatars, and pink only in the success state.

7 · Compare and edit

Compare: add ?people=old to any page (for example es/07c-share.html?people=old), or use the “Person and share-sheet colors” switch in the toolbar of every page and on the screen grid. It switches the person colors and the share sheet together; ?share=old switches the share sheet only. The proposed colors are the default.
